What we offer

Services

Readers' Haven gives every reader a quiet place to read, learn and grow. These are the services you can use with your library membership.

Book Borrowing

Members can borrow books from our collection and return them before the due date. Borrowing limits and loan periods depend on your membership plan.

Most popular

Reading Room

Our reading room is a quiet place for students and readers, with free Wi-Fi and charging points. It is open every day of the week.

Digital Library

Members can read e-books and online journals from home. Ask at the front desk for your digital library login.

Study Groups and Events

We hold weekly study groups, book clubs and author talks. New events are announced on the notice board and on our website.

Help Desk

Our staff can help you find a book, renew your membership, or pay a late fee. Visit the help desk or contact us through the contact page.
