Membership

A Readers' Haven card lets you borrow from the shelves, use the reading room, and join study groups. Choose one plan below. Every fee is for one year and is paid in Pakistani rupees at the help desk.

Reader

For students and first-time members.

PKR 1,500 annual fee

Borrowing limit
2 books at a time.
Loan period
14 days from the day you borrow.
Late return
PKR 10 per book for each day after the due date. If a book is still out 14 days after it was due, the card is paused until the book is back and the fee is paid.

Family

One card shared by a household.

PKR 3,500 annual fee

Shared household card
Borrowing limit
6 books at a time, shared by everyone named on the card.
Loan period
21 days from the day you borrow.
Late return
PKR 15 per book for each day after the due date. Ask before the due date for one free 7-day renewal. A paused card stops borrowing for every person named on it.

Scholar

For researchers and heavy borrowers.

PKR 6,000 annual fee

Borrowing limit
8 books at a time, including up to 2 short-loan reference copies.
Loan period
28 days for ordinary books. Reference copies must come back in 3 days.
Late return
PKR 20 per ordinary book for each day after the due date. A late reference copy is PKR 50 per day. A lost book is charged at the current replacement price plus a PKR 400 processing fee.

How to join

  1. Bring a CNIC, or a student card if you are choosing the Reader plan, to the help desk.
  2. Pick a plan and pay the annual fee in PKR. Family cards need the name of each person who will borrow.
  3. Collect your card the same day. It is valid for one year from the date of payment.

Questions about a fee or a late book can go to the help desk, or through the contact page.
